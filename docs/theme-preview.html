GISR theme options

Four site themes selectable from account preferences. Green / Teal is the default; Dark Navy and Dark Grey reuse its layout with a different palette, and GISR Hub is derived from the HUD template. Same content in each; only tokens and effects differ.

HUD traits reproduced in the GISR Hub theme

  • Page background #1d2835 with a vertical gradient from rgba(50,70,80,.9) to #0d101b.
  • Left nav and main content have no background and the nav has no right border; nav links are 50% white, turning white on hover and current.
  • Top bar rgba(29,40,53,.95) with no bottom border.
  • Cards are transparent with a thin crossing-line frame (inset 15px, 30% white) and L-shaped corner brackets (75% white). The highlighted card uses the blue accent.
  • Primary button #0271ff, hover #0255bf, disabled #4195ff at 65% opacity; links #4e9cff, link hover #9ac6ff.
  • Transparent form fields with a 25% white border; focus is a 50% white border plus a 25% white ring.
  • 4px corner radius, Chakra Petch at weight 300 with 0.75px letter spacing (loaded from Google Fonts in this preview only).
